# Workshop 2.2: Regional Digital Cooperation (Caribbean)
## Phase 2: Digital Collaboration

**Workshop Code:** WS 2.2
**Primary Component:** Interoperability Platform + e-ID (Regional Extension)
**Duration:** 2.5 days
**Timing:** Month 5, Week 2-4
**Phase Theme:** "Connecting Systems, Connecting Nations"

---

## Executive Summary

This pivotal workshop transforms Trinidad & Tobago's digital transformation from a national initiative into a regional leadership opportunity. By bringing together 2-3 Caribbean partner countries, the workshop explores how the interoperability platform and e-ID system designed in earlier workshops can enable cross-border services, mutual recognition of digital credentials, and shared digital infrastructure. This workshop positions T&T as the Caribbean's digital hub and creates a foundation for CARICOM-wide digital integration, drawing lessons from the EU's eIDAS framework and Nordic digital cooperation models.

**Key Outcome:** Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) or Letters of Intent for regional digital cooperation, with concrete use cases for cross-border interoperability and e-ID recognition.

---

## Workshop Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Regional Partnership Formation:** Establish formal cooperation agreements with 2-3 Caribbean countries for digital integration
2. **Cross-Border Use Cases:** Identify and design high-value cross-border services (education, professional licensing, travel, business)
3. **Technical Interoperability:** Extend national interoperability platform architecture to support regional data exchange
4. **Mutual e-ID Recognition:** Design framework for cross-border recognition of digital identities
5. **Knowledge Sharing:** Transfer T&T learnings and EU best practices to Caribbean partners

### Secondary Objectives
- Build Caribbean coalition for digital transformation
- Explore data centre regional services opportunities
- Establish regional governance mechanisms
- Create replication framework for other Caribbean nations
- Position CARICOM as digitally integrated region

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Partner countries don't commit/attend | Early outreach at highest levels; demonstrate value; start with 2 countries, add 3rd if possible |
| Political tensions between countries | Focus on technical cooperation; emphasize mutual benefits; CAF/EU as neutral facilitators |
| Different maturity levels create imbalance | Design pilots where T&T can support less-advanced partners; phased approach allows catch-up |
| Legal/sovereignty concerns block agreement | Start with non-binding LOI; technical pilots before full legal framework; respect each country's pace |
| Unrealistic expectations about timeline/scope | Clear roadmap with phases; quick wins plus long-term vision; manage expectations actively |
| Funding uncertainty for regional infrastructure | Explore CAF regional financing; cost-sharing models; start with low-cost pilots |
