# Workshop 3.3: Private Sector & Third-Party Engagement
## Phase 3: Digital Innovation

**Workshop Code:** WS 3.3
**Primary Component:** Data Centre Expansion (Third-Party Usage)
**Duration:** 1.5 days
**Timing:** Month 9, Week 2
**Phase Theme:** "Empowering the Digital Ecosystem"

---

## Executive Summary

This workshop opens the tech hub and digital infrastructure to responsible private sector and third-party use, transforming government infrastructure into a platform for broader economic development. Participants will design frameworks for authorized third-party access, establish commercial models, create security and compliance requirements, and identify partnership opportunities. This workshop balances the imperative to drive digital economy growth with the responsibility to protect public assets and citizen data.

**Key Outcome:** Third-party engagement framework with clear policies, security standards, commercial models, and initial partnership agreements.

---

## Workshop Objectives

1. **Define Access Framework:** Establish who can access government digital infrastructure and under what terms
2. **Commercial Models:** Design sustainable pricing and partnership structures
3. **Security & Compliance:** Create rigorous vetting and ongoing compliance requirements
4. **Partnership Opportunities:** Identify and engage potential private sector partners
5. **Public-Private Collaboration:** Design mechanisms for co-innovation and knowledge exchange

## Detailed Agenda

### DAY 1: Frameworks & Models

#### Morning: Strategic Vision & Access Framework (9:00 AM - 12:30 PM)

**Opening (30 min)**
- Vision: Government infrastructure as economic development platform
- Benefits: Innovation, jobs, competitiveness, revenue
- Risks: Security, misuse, inequity, dependency

**Presentation: Global PPP Models (40 min)**
- Estonia: E-residency as business platform
- Singapore: Government as platform (GaaP)
- EU public-private digital partnerships
- Open banking APIs (PSD2 model)

**Access Framework Workshop (90 min)**
Breakout groups design access policies:
- Who qualifies? (Criteria: legal status, security clearance, use case approval)
- What can they access? (Sandboxes vs. production infrastructure, data vs. compute, APIs vs. raw infrastructure)
- Terms and conditions (acceptable use, prohibited activities, data handling, IP ownership)
- Approval process (application, vetting, contracts, ongoing compliance)
- Termination conditions (violations, security incidents, insolvency)

**12:30 - 1:30 PM: LUNCH**

#### Afternoon: Commercial Models & Sustainability (1:30 PM - 5:00 PM)

**Presentation: Pricing & Business Models (30 min)**
- Free tier (startups, research, public good projects)
- Cost-recovery tier (cover infrastructure costs)
- Commercial tier (market-rate for profit-making use)
- Partnership/co-investment models
- In-kind contributions (expertise, technology transfer, training)

**Business Model Workshop (90 min)**
Develop pricing framework:
- Service catalogue (compute, storage, APIs, data, support)
- Pricing tiers and structure
- Revenue projections
- Reinvestment model (how revenue funds further innovation)
- Special programs (startup discounts, social enterprise support, academic rates)

**Private Sector Panel (40 min)**
What would make government infrastructure attractive to companies?
What barriers exist? What pricing is fair? What support is needed?

**Day 1 Synthesis (20 min)**

---

### DAY 2 (Half Day): Security, Partnerships & Launch

#### Morning Only (9:00 AM - 1:00 PM)

**Security & Compliance Framework (90 min)**
- Vetting requirements (security audits, background checks, financial stability)
- Technical security (network isolation, access controls, monitoring, audit logging)
- Data protection compliance (GDPR alignment, citizen data prohibitions)
- Incident response (breach notification, remediation, penalties)
- Ongoing compliance (periodic audits, performance reviews)
- Enforcement (suspension, termination, blacklisting)

**Partnership Opportunities Workshop (75 min)**
Identify and design specific partnerships:
- **Startup Incubation:** GovTech accelerator program
- **Co-Innovation:** Joint pilots on specific challenges
- **Technology Transfer:** International companies share expertise
- **Training Partnerships:** Companies provide skills development
- **Regional Services:** Companies using T&T hub to serve Caribbean market
- **Open Innovation:** Challenges with prizes for solutions

Develop term sheets for 2-3 priority partnerships

**Action Planning & Launch Preparation (30 min)**
- Immediate next steps (finalize policies, legal templates, marketing)
- Launch event planning (Month 9-10)
- First partners onboarding timeline
- Metrics and evaluation

**Closing (15 min)**

---

## Key Discussion Questions

- How do we balance openness with security and control?
- What pricing is fair—cost recovery, market rate, or subsidized?
- How do we prevent private sector capture of public infrastructure?
- How do we ensure SMEs and startups can access, not just large companies?
- What happens if a private partner misuses infrastructure or data?
- How do we measure success—revenue, jobs, innovation, or all three?
