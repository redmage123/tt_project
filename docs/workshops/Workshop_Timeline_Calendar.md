## Detailed Monthly Schedule

### PHASE 1: DIGITAL FOUNDATIONS

#### Month 1 - Week 3-4
**Workshop 1.1: Digital Identity Ecosystem Kickoff**
- **Duration:** 2 days
- **Primary Component:** e-ID Program
- **Key Stakeholders:** MPAAI, 4 ID providers, MDAs, National-CSIRT, 2-3 Caribbean agencies
- **Deliverable Link:** Product 3 (Workshop Reports)

#### Month 2 - Week 2-3
**Workshop 1.2: Data Centre as National Infrastructure**
- **Duration:** 1.5 days
- **Primary Component:** Data Centre Expansion
- **Key Stakeholders:** MPAAI, Data Centre team, Infrastructure providers, EU experts
- **Deliverable Link:** Product 3 (Workshop Reports)

#### Month 3 - Week 2-3
**Workshop 1.3: Gender, Inclusion & Digital Rights Foundation**
- **Duration:** 1.5 days
- **Primary Component:** Cross-cutting (All)
- **Key Stakeholders:** MPAAI, Civil society, Women's groups, Disability advocates, MDAs

**End of Q1 Milestone:** Product 2 - Technical Progress Analysis 1

## Flexibility & Contingency

### Buffer Time
- 1-2 week buffers built into schedule between intensive workshops
- Month 12 reserved for final reporting with no workshops scheduled

### Contingency Scenarios

**If a workshop must be rescheduled:**
1. **Phase 1 workshops** - Can shift 1-2 weeks within their month
2. **Phase 2 workshops** - WS 2.2 requires international coordination; book early
3. **Phase 3 workshops** - WS 3.2 is flexible (can be split into multiple sessions)
4. **Phase 4 workshops** - WS 4.2 must complete before Month 11 end for final reporting

**If regional partners can't attend specific workshop:**
- WS 2.2 is critical - reschedule if necessary
- WS 1.1 and 3.1 are beneficial but not essential for partner attendance

## Pre-Workshop Checklist Timeline

**6-8 Weeks Before:**
- Confirm date and venue
- Invite regional/international participants
- Book EU experts

**4 Weeks Before:**
- Finalize agenda
- Confirm local stakeholder participation
- Begin content development

**2 Weeks Before:**
- Complete presentation materials
- Arrange technical setup
- Send pre-workshop materials to participants

**1 Week Before:**
- Final confirmation from all participants
- Technical rehearsal
- Print materials

---

## Post-Workshop Activities Timeline

**Week 1 After:**
- Gather feedback surveys
- Draft workshop report

**Week 2 After:**
- Finalize workshop report (Product 3)
- Share with stakeholders
- Begin implementing workshop recommendations

**Week 3-4 After:**
- Update project plans based on workshop outcomes
- Prepare for next workshop
