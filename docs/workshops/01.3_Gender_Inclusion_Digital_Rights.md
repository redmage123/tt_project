# Workshop 1.3: Gender, Inclusion & Digital Rights Foundation
## Phase 1: Digital Foundations

**Workshop Code:** WS 1.3
**Primary Component:** Cross-cutting (All Components)
**Duration:** 1.5 days
**Timing:** Month 3, Week 2-3
**Phase Theme:** "Building Trust & Infrastructure"

---

## Executive Summary

This critical workshop embeds gender, inclusion, and digital rights principles into the DNA of Trinidad & Tobago's digital transformation from the outset. Rather than treating these as add-ons, the workshop establishes frameworks and commitments that will be woven through all subsequent project activities. Participants will explore inclusive design principles, accessibility standards, privacy protections, and strategies to ensure that e-ID, interoperability, and data centre initiatives serve all citizens equitably—particularly marginalized populations including women, youth, persons with disabilities, and those facing economic barriers.

**Key Outcome:** Actionable Gender, Inclusion, and Diversity (GID) framework integrated into all three project components with measurable commitments.

---

## Workshop Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Establish GID Framework:** Create practical framework for embedding gender and inclusion principles across all project components
2. **Inclusive Design Standards:** Define accessibility and universal design requirements for e-ID and digital services
3. **Digital Rights Protections:** Align with international standards on privacy, data protection, and digital rights
4. **Address Digital Divides:** Identify barriers to digital access and develop mitigation strategies
5. **Empower Vulnerable Populations:** Design specific interventions for women facing GBV, persons with disabilities, elderly, rural populations

### Secondary Objectives
- Learn from EU best practices (GDPR, accessibility directives)
- Build capacity within project team on GID principles
- Establish monitoring and accountability mechanisms
- Create stakeholder coalition for ongoing advocacy

## Detailed Agenda

### DAY 1: Frameworks, Rights & Inclusive Design

- **Presentation:** "Digital Rights as Human Rights" (30 min)
  - UN frameworks (Universal Declaration of Human Rights in digital age)
  - Right to privacy
  - Right to non-discrimination
  - Right to access information
  - Data protection as fundamental right

- **Presentation:** "EU Digital Rights Framework" (25 min)
  - GDPR principles and applicability
  - EU Accessibility Directive

  - Lessons for Trinidad & Tobago

- **Interactive Discussion:** "T&T Digital Rights Charter" (20 min)

**10:45 - 11:00 | BREAK**

**11:00 - 12:30 | Gender & Digital Transformation**
- **Presentation:** "Gender Dimensions of Digital Identity" (25 min)

  - Identity systems and gender-based violence (privacy concerns)
  - Transgender and non-binary considerations
  - Economic empowerment through digital access
  - Women in tech sector development

- **Case Studies:** "Gender-Responsive Digital Services" (30 min)
  - Estonia: Secure access to services for abuse survivors

  - **Group 1:** e-ID program gender implications
  - **Group 2:** Interoperability and women's service access
  - **Group 3:** Data centre jobs and skills (gender equity)
  - **Group 4:** Privacy and safety for vulnerable women

  Each group identifies:
  - Potential risks/harms
  - Opportunities for empowerment
  - Design requirements
  - Safeguards needed

**12:30 - 1:30 PM | LUNCH**

**1:30 - 3:15 | Accessibility & Universal Design**
- **Presentation:** "Universal Design for Digital Services" (30 min)
  - Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1)
  - Assistive technologies (screen readers, voice recognition)

  - Low-literacy design
  - Multi-modal interfaces

    - Training programs (gender-sensitive)

## Key Discussion Questions

### Rights & Ethics Questions
- How do we balance identity verification security with privacy rights?
- What safeguards prevent digital identity from becoming surveillance?
- How do we protect the most vulnerable while providing universal access?
- What consent mechanisms are truly informed and freely given?

### Gender Questions
- How can digital identity empower women economically and socially?
- What specific risks do women face with digital identity systems?
- How do we design for transgender and non-binary individuals?
- How can we ensure women's participation in tech jobs created by the project?

### Accessibility Questions
- What does "accessible to all" truly mean in the T&T context?
- How do we serve populations with low digital literacy?
- What offline alternatives must be maintained?
- How do we design for cognitive and learning disabilities, not just physical?

### Implementation Questions
- How do we embed these principles in procurement and contracts?
- What training does the project team need on GID?
- How do we measure success on inclusion goals?
- How do we ensure this isn't just a checkbox exercise?

## Gender & Inclusion Considerations

### Representation (THE POINT OF THIS WORKSHOP)
- At least 50% women participants

- Safe space agreements
