# Workshop 3.4: MDA Digital Transformation Showcase (Part 1)
## Phase 3: Digital Innovation

**Workshop Code:** WS 3.4
**Primary Component:** Interoperability Platform + Data Centre (MDA Engagement #1)
**Duration:** 1 day
**Timing:** Month 9, Week 4
**Phase Theme:** "Empowering the Digital Ecosystem"

---

## Executive Summary

This is the first of two MDA showcase events designed to accelerate adoption of the interoperability platform, data centre infrastructure, and digital transformation tools across government. Early adopter agencies demonstrate their progress, lessons learned, and benefits realized. The event builds momentum, addresses concerns, provides peer learning, and motivates additional MDAs to join the digital ecosystem.

**Key Outcome:** Increased MDA commitment to platform adoption with concrete onboarding plans for 5-10 additional agencies.

---

## Workshop Objectives

1. **Demonstrate Value:** Early adopters showcase working solutions and tangible benefits
2. **Peer Learning:** MDAs learn from each other's experiences
3. **Address Barriers:** Identify and solve common obstacles to adoption
4. **Accelerate Adoption:** New MDAs commit to onboarding timelines
5. **Build Community:** Create network of digital champions across government

---

## Target Participants (60-80 people)

### All MDAs (50-60)
- IT directors/CIOs from all ministries, departments, agencies
- Digital transformation leads
- Line managers interested in service improvement
- At least 2-3 representatives per MDA

### Early Adopter Presenters (8-12)
- Agencies that have implemented pilots or early integrations
- Technical staff and business owners who can speak to results

### Leadership & Support (10-12)
- MPAAI Minister or Permanent Secretary (opening)
- Project team
- CAF and EU Delegation
- National-CSIRT
- Technical support staff

---

## Detailed Agenda (Single Day)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:30 PM)

**Opening (30 min)**
- Ministerial welcome: "Whole-of-Government Digital Transformation"
- Project progress overview (e-ID, interoperability, data centre, regional pilots)
- Vision: Connected, efficient, citizen-centric government

**Keynote: "The Interoperable Government" (30 min)**
- EU expert presents successful government-wide digital transformation
- Estonian X-Road ecosystem with 99% of services digital
- Benefits: Speed, accuracy, cost savings, citizen satisfaction
- How to achieve: Leadership, standards, community, persistence

**Early Adopter Demonstrations (90 min)**
6 early adopter agencies (15 min each):

**Possible Examples:**
1. **Health:** Patient records accessible across facilities
   - Use case, technical approach, challenges, results
2. **Education:** Student records transfer between institutions
3. **Revenue Authority:** Automated business registration data exchange
4. **Social Services:** Integrated eligibility verification
5. **Immigration:** Background check integration
6. **Land Registry:** Property verification API for mortgages

Each demo shows:
- Problem solved
- Solution implemented (live if possible)
- Integration with interoperability platform
- Benefits (time saved, errors reduced, user satisfaction)
- Lessons learned

**Q&A (30 min)**

**12:30 - 1:30 PM: LUNCH & NETWORKING**

### Afternoon Session (1:30 PM - 4:30 PM)

**Panel: "Lessons from the Trenches" (45 min)**
Early adopters discuss:
- Biggest challenges (technical, organizational, change management)
- How they overcame them
- What they wish they'd known
- Advice for agencies just starting
- Importance of executive sponsorship

**Workshop: "Planning Your Adoption" (90 min)**
MDAs not yet onboarded work in groups:
- Identify services that could benefit from integration
- Map current process vs. digital-enabled process
- Estimate benefits (time, cost, satisfaction)
- Identify data needed from other agencies
- Identify data they can provide to others
- Draft integration roadmap (6-12 months)
- Identify resources and support needed

MDA groups present their plans (5 min each)

**Interoperability Platform Deep Dive (30 min)**
Technical overview for interested agencies:
- How to connect to the platform
- API standards and documentation
- Security and authentication
- Testing and sandbox environments
- Support available

**Commitments & Next Steps (20 min)**
- MDAs publicly commit to next steps (formal or informal)
- MPAAI commits to support and resources
- Timeline for MDA onboarding cohorts
- Preview of Part 2 showcase (Month 10)

**Closing Remarks (5 min)**
