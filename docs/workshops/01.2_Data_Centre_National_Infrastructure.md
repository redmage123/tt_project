# Workshop 1.2: Data Centre as National Infrastructure
## Phase 1: Digital Foundations

**Workshop Code:** WS 1.2
**Primary Component:** Data Centre Expansion
**Duration:** 1.5 days
**Timing:** Month 2, Week 2-3
**Phase Theme:** "Building Trust & Infrastructure"

---

## Executive Summary

This workshop establishes the strategic vision for Trinidad & Tobago's Tier-4 Government Data Centre as critical national infrastructure that will serve as the foundation for digital transformation. Moving beyond traditional hosting services, the workshop positions the data centre as a catalyst for innovation, a regional digital hub, and an enabler of public-private partnerships. Participants will explore hybrid cloud strategies, green infrastructure approaches, and the data centre's evolution into a technology sandbox supporting AI, ML, blockchain, and IoT initiatives.

**Key Outcome:** Consensus on data centre expansion strategy, hybrid cloud architecture, and pathway to becoming a regional tech hub.

---

## Workshop Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Strategic Positioning:** Establish data centre as foundational infrastructure for e-ID, interoperability, and innovation
2. **Hybrid Cloud Strategy:** Define optimal balance between on-premise Tier-4 infrastructure and cloud services
3. **Green & Inclusive Operations:** Design for environmental sustainability and equitable access
4. **Regional Hub Vision:** Position T&T data centre as Caribbean resource
5. **Innovation Platform:** Plan for tech hub and sandbox environments (AI/ML/blockchain/IoT)

### Secondary Objectives
- Build understanding of Tier-4 data centre standards and requirements
- Learn from EU best practices in government data centres
- Establish data sovereignty and security frameworks
- Identify capacity building needs for operations and management
- Begin vendor engagement strategy

## Key Discussion Questions

### Strategic Questions
- What role should the government data centre play in T&T's digital economy?
- Should the data centre serve regional Caribbean needs? Under what model?
- How do we balance data sovereignty with cost-effective cloud services?
- What is the business model for sustainability (cost recovery, revenue generation)?

### Technical Questions
- What is the right balance between Tier-4 on-premise and public cloud?
- How do we ensure 99.995% uptime in the T&T context (power, connectivity)?
- What redundancy is truly necessary vs. nice-to-have?
- How do we future-proof for emerging technologies (quantum, edge computing)?

### Sustainability Questions
- What is a realistic PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) target for tropical climate?
- Can we achieve carbon neutrality? By when?
- What renewable energy options are viable in Trinidad & Tobago?
- How do we measure and report on environmental impact?

### Implementation Questions
- What can be accomplished in the 12-month project timeframe?
- What requires longer-term investment beyond this project?
- How do we phase implementation to deliver value early?
- What are the critical procurement decisions?

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Vendor presentations become sales pitches | Pre-approve all content; strict time limits; frame as "market education" |
| Technical complexity overwhelms non-technical participants | Balance technical depth with strategic content; use analogies; provide glossary |
| Unrealistic sustainability targets | Ground in EU case studies with similar climate/context; expert guidance on feasibility |
| Scope creep (trying to solve everything) | Clear workshop scope; parking lot for out-of-scope items; phased roadmap |
| Lack of consensus on cloud strategy | Present as framework, not final decision; emphasize flexibility and evolution |
