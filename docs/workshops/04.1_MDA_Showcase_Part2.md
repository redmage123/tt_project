# Workshop 4.1: MDA Digital Transformation Showcase (Part 2)
## Phase 4: Digital Sustainability

**Workshop Code:** WS 4.1
**Primary Component:** Interoperability Platform + Data Centre (MDA Engagement #2)
**Duration:** 1 day
**Timing:** Month 10, Week 3
**Phase Theme:** "Scaling, Securing, and Sustaining"

---

## Executive Summary

Building on the momentum from Workshop 3.4, this second showcase demonstrates expanded adoption, mature implementations, and quantified results. Agencies that committed in Part 1 present their progress. The focus shifts from "why" and "how" to "results" and "what's next"—solidifying the interoperability platform and tech hub as core government infrastructure.

**Key Outcome:** Whole-of-government commitment to digital transformation with long-term sustainability plans and governance transition.

---

## Workshop Objectives

1. **Demonstrate Maturity:** Show evolution from pilots to production services
2. **Quantify Impact:** Present measurable results (time, cost, satisfaction, outcomes)
3. **Expand Adoption:** Additional MDAs commit to onboarding
4. **Sustainability Planning:** Transition from project mode to operational governance
5. **Celebrate Success:** Recognize achievements and build esprit de corps

---

## Target Participants (70-90 people)

### All MDAs (60-70)
- Broader participation than Part 1
- Senior leadership (Permanent Secretaries encouraged to attend)
- IT directors and line managers
- Agencies from Part 1 return to see progress

### New Presenters (12-15)
- Agencies that onboarded since Part 1
- Original early adopters showing matured implementations
- Regional pilot participants (if available)

### Leadership & Guests (10-15)
- Minister of Public Administration and AI (opening/closing)
- CAF and EU senior representatives
- Media (if public component)
- Selected private sector partners (from WS 3.3)

---

## Detailed Agenda (Single Day)

### Morning Session (9:00 AM - 12:30 PM)

**Opening & Progress Update (30 min)**
- Ministerial remarks celebrating progress
- Project overview: "What We've Accomplished in 10 Months"
  - E-ID status (users, services)
  - Interoperability platform (agencies connected, APIs, transactions)
  - Data centre/tech hub (utilization, projects, partnerships)
  - Regional pilots (cross-border services launched)
- By the numbers: Quantified impact across all initiatives

**Success Stories (90 min)**
8-10 agencies (8-10 min each) showcase results:

**Focus on Metrics:**
- Time savings (permit processing time reduced X%, service delivery accelerated X days)
- Cost savings (eliminated redundant data entry, reduced manual processes)
- Error reduction (data accuracy improved, inconsistencies eliminated)
- User satisfaction (citizen feedback, employee feedback)
- New services enabled (services that weren't possible before integration)

**Examples might include:**
1. **Original early adopters:** Showing scaled implementations and long-term results
2. **Part 1 committed agencies:** Demonstrating they followed through
3. **New adopters:** Fresh perspectives and quick wins
4. **Cross-agency collaboration:** Services requiring multiple agencies working together
5. **Regional success:** Cross-border service in operation (if ready)

**Interactive Q&A (30 min)**
- Audience questions to presenters
- Peer-to-peer learning

**12:30 - 1:30 PM: LUNCH & DEMOS**
- Exhibition area with live demonstrations
- Hands-on exploration of services
- Networking

### Afternoon Session (1:30 PM - 4:30 PM)

**Panel: "From Project to Operations" (45 min)**
MPAAI leadership, early adopters, technical team discuss:
- Transitioning from pilot to production to standard practice
- Sustaining momentum when project funding ends
- Governance transition (from project steering to operational oversight)
- Continuous improvement and evolution
- Long-term funding and resource allocation
- Knowledge retention and skills development

**Workshop: "The Next Wave" (60 min)**
MDAs not yet fully onboarded:
- Review their progress since Part 1 (if applicable)
- Identify additional integration opportunities
- Plan for next 6-12 months
- Form peer learning cohorts
- Commit to timelines

New use cases and innovation ideas:
- What new services could we create now that platform is mature?
- How can we leverage tech hub for service innovation?
- Regional expansion opportunities

**Looking Forward: Integration into National Digital Strategy (30 min)**
- How does this project fit into broader digital government agenda?
- What comes after the project ends (Month 12)?
- Roadmap for years 2-5
- Sustainability and governance model (preview WS 4.2)

**Recognition & Celebration (15 min)**
- Awards/recognition for exemplary adopters
- Team acknowledgments
- Community building

**Closing Remarks (10 min)**
- Ministerial closing: Vision for T&T as digital government leader
- Thanks to all participants
- Preview of final workshop (WS 4.2: Governance)
